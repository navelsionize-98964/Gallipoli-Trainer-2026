## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Gallipoli**. This trainer provides optional assistance for offline matches and bot-supported custom sessions. It can protect the player from damage, replenish ammunition, remove reload delays, improve weapon handling, and increase movement speed without changing Gallipoli's core class-based combat structure.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to practice classes, explore Gallipoli's maps, test weapons, or experiment with objective-based battles against bots without affecting competitive multiplayer.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Gallipoli uses deliberate first-person movement suited to trench warfare, beach assaults, desert expeditions, and urban combat. Use cover, terrain, and squad positioning to move between objectives. The Fast Movement option can accelerate traversal during offline practice, but excessive values may cause clipping or instability.

### Tools & Inventory

Gallipoli does not use a traditional RPG inventory. Before deployment, select a historical class and its available weapons and equipment. The trainer's ammunition options affect the active loadout rather than creating new equipment or unlocking classes.

### World & Environment

Battles take place across the Gallipoli Peninsula and Mesopotamian theatres, including coastal ravines, beaches, deserts, forts, and urban areas. Matches focus on capturing or defending objectives, with bots available in supported public and custom sessions.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is built around one-shot-one-kill lethality, weighty firearms, methodical reloads, suppression, and teamwork. Players can choose from ten specialized historical classes, including Officer, Heavy Machine Gunner, Sniper, and Stretcher Bearer. Weapon familiarity improves aiming, handling, and reloading under pressure. Trainer options should be activated before or during an offline engagement and disabled before entering online play.
